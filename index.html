Lecturer, Dept. of CSE, United International University • Robotics, Embedded Systems & Autonomous Systems Engineer

Robotics, Embedded Systems & Autonomous Systems engineer, researcher, and robotics educator with hands-on experience spanning embedded firmware, sensor interfacing, autonomous robotic platforms, IoT systems, computer vision, and AI/ML. Experienced in designing and implementing basic hardware circuits, developing firmware and custom sensor libraries for NVIDIA embedded platforms, ROS2-based autonomous navigation, multi-sensor fusion, path planning, robotic manipulation, and real-time IoT monitoring. Co-Team Lead of the UIU Mars Rover Team with international competition experience and approximately three years of hands-on technical training and guidance of robotics team members and interns.
